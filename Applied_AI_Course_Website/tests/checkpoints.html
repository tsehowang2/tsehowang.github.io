Checkpoint quizzes

Short diagnostic checks after weeks 4 and 8. Attempt them before opening the solution notes.

Checkpoint A after week 4

Twenty minutes. (1) Estimate next-word probabilities from three bus, one train and one bicycle sentences. (2) Use vocabulary I=0, go=1, to=2, school=3, by=4, bus=5. For each prefix of “I go to school by bus” ending before bus, give its next-token target ID. (3) Compare dot product and cosine of [1,0] with [2,0] and [1,1]. (4) Compute softmax and target negative log likelihood for two equal logits. Repair missing concepts before week 5.

Checkpoint B after week 8

Twenty minutes. (1) Mix [2,0] and [0,4] with weights .25 and .75. (2) Give Q/K/V and score shapes for T=8,d_model=32,d_head=16. (3) Compare masking scores before softmax with zeroing probabilities after softmax, with and without renormalization. (4) Describe a future-token perturbation check. Repair missing concepts before week 9.

Show checkpoint calculation guide

A1: [.6,.2,.2]. A2: I→go(1); I go→to(2); I go to→school(3); I go to school→by(4); I go to school by→bus(5). A3: dot products 2 and 1; cosines 1 and 1/sqrt(2). A4: [.5,.5], loss ln(2)=.6931. B1: [.5,3]. B2: Q/K/V [8,16], scores [8,8], output [8,16]. B3: pre-softmax masking gives zero probability to forbidden positions. Post-softmax zeroing without renormalization shrinks total mass; zeroing then renormalizing is equivalent for a nonempty allowed set, up to numerical error. B4: keep model fixed in eval mode, change a future token, compare earlier outputs within numerical tolerance.
