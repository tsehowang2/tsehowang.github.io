Question 2

For B=2,T=5,d_model=8,H=2,V=20, give IDs, head-Q, scores, and final-logit shapes.

Show worked solution

IDs=[2,5]; Q=[2,2,5,4]; scores=[2,2,5,5]; logits=[2,5,20].

Question 3

A model improves loss after unmasking and training unshifted labels. Explain why that is inconclusive and propose checks.

Show worked solution

It may exploit future targets or learn same-token copying. Print input/targets, test causal perturbation, evaluate proper held-out next-token loss and generation. Lower loss on the wrong task is not improvement.
