Question 1

Distinguish retrieval success, groundedness and task success using one documentation question.

Show worked solution

Retrieval finds the relevant versioned timeout passage; grounding means answer claims follow from it; task success means the answer actually gives the requested setting and usable context. One can occur without the others.

Question 2

A system answers 15/20 tasks and 12 answers are correct. Calculate answered-question accuracy and coverage. What else is needed for overall correctness?

Show worked solution

Accuracy among answers=12/15=80%; coverage=15/20=75%. Need labels for the five refusals and a definition of task-level correctness to count them.

Question 3

Propose a five-class task taxonomy and one measurable requirement per class for your assistant.

Show worked solution

Example: lookup/correct fact+source; comparison/both versions; troubleshooting/supported resolution; calculation/verified value; unsupported/appropriate insufficiency. Award one per justified measurable class, capped at four.
