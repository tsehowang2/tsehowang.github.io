How the course works

A clear path from
intuition to evidence.

Each week opens with an ordinary example, develops the mechanism and then goes deeper. Detailed HTML slides sit beside complete lecture notes, a guided tutorial and worked practice questions.

Semester one is for language models and transformers. Semester two turns that understanding into a documentation assistant, from search to evaluation, tools and release.
